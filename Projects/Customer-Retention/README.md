# Customer Retention & Cohorts (Case Study)

## Business Question

Which acquisition channels retain customers the longest, and where should marketing budget shift?

## Data Sources

- **Customers**: signup date, acquisition channel, geography.
- **Orders**: transaction-level purchases linked to customers.
- **Marketing Spend**: channel-level spend by month.

> Replace the datasets above with your actual sources and note any limitations.

## Methodology (Simple Overview)

1. Built monthly cohorts based on **first purchase date**.
2. Calculated **retention rate** by cohort month and channel.
3. Compared **retained revenue** to **acquisition cost**.

## Key Insights (Plain Language)

- **Organic search retains best**: customers from organic search return more often after month 2.
- **Paid social drops quickly**: high first-month volume but lower repeat behavior.
- **Email performs steadily**: consistent retention with moderate acquisition cost.

## Recommendations

- Shift budget toward organic + email programs to improve long-term value.
- Reduce paid social spend until onboarding improves repeat purchase rate.
- Add lifecycle email campaigns for month-2 customers to boost retention.

## Next Steps

- Segment retention by customer value tier.
- Test new onboarding flows for paid social cohorts.
