# Marketing Funnel Optimization (Case Study)

## Business Question

Where does the funnel lose the most qualified leads, and which channel should we prioritize?

## Data Sources

- **Web Sessions**: visits, channel, landing page.
- **Leads**: lead creation date, channel, lead score.
- **Opportunities**: stage, win/loss status, revenue.

> Replace the datasets above with your actual sources and note any limitations.

## Methodology (Simple Overview)

1. Mapped funnel stages: **Visit → Lead → Qualified → Won**.
2. Calculated conversion rates by channel.
3. Compared conversion rates to average deal value.

## Key Insights (Plain Language)

- **Paid search brings volume but weak qualification**, so many leads stall early.
- **Partner referrals convert best**, with the highest win rate and deal size.
- **Content marketing grows steadily**, but needs stronger lead qualification.

## Recommendations

- Invest more in partner referral programs.
- Improve paid search targeting to reduce low-quality leads.
- Add lead scoring improvements for content-driven leads.

## Next Steps

- A/B test landing pages for paid search.
- Review lead qualification criteria with sales.
